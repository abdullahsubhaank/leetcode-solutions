# Problem: Reverse Linked List (Easy)
**Link:** [https://leetcode.com/problems/reverse-linked-list/](https://leetcode.com/problems/reverse-linked-list/)

### Approach
In-place iterative 3-pointer reversal. We reverse a singly-linked list in-place iteratively by tracking the `prev`, `curr`, and `next_node`.
### Complexity
- **Time:** O(n)
- **Space:** O(1)
